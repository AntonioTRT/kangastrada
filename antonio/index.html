How I got here

Engineering has always been about making things work.

I’m originally from Mexico and later moved to Finland. Engineering became both my profession and a way to stay curious: understand a problem, get hands-on, test an idea, and keep improving it.

My work spans medical and wearable devices, battery-powered products, embedded systems, product verification, and support for manufacturing. I enjoy being involved across the product lifecycle, from early concepts through validation and production.

Outside work, I’m into technology, heavy metal, music, audio and DSP, and fitness. I also use AI as a practical tool for research, debugging, automation, and documentation.
